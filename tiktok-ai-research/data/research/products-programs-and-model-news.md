# Products, programs and model news: what to consider

Theme: `products-programs-and-model-news` (29 liked videos, categories ai-product-tool + model-news), plus five cross-theme videos the brief asked about (Claude for Startups x2, Max/Team API credits, MarkItDown, Hark). Research date: 2026-10-10. Verification was done against official pages wherever the session proxy allowed (claude.com, support.claude.com, platform.claude.com, code.claude.com, github.com); where a vendor domain was unreachable (NVIDIA, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Apify, Granola, wajo.ai, veteranai.co, hark.ai, labs.google, typesafe.ai) the row says so and leans on dated secondary coverage. Web search budget ran out before Postiz cloud pricing, OpenAI's Build/Launch/Grow tier names, Avori and the Karen Times bot could be pinned down; those are marked unverified.

## What the creators are collectively saying (patterns, not individual videos)

1. **"Free" is the hook, the fine print is the story.** Eleven of the 34 items are pitched as free: NVIDIA endpoints, Postiz, Scrapling, Voicebox, MarkItDown, MiroFish, open-slide, Little Language Lessons, Cue, Fo, Muse, Instinct, the Claude credits. On inspection the genuinely free-and-durable ones are the open-source repos and the two Anthropic programs; the rest are trials, invite-only early access, rate-limited sandboxes, or self-host-only.
2. **The same four creators recycle one format.** @bengusberg ("Stanford founder, raised $4M") posted five of the 29: NVIDIA, Postiz, Scrapling, Apify, Little Language Lessons, all "paste the GitHub URL into Claude and ask it to install, 20-second setup". @nocode.joshua did two listicles (Claude Motion with a mocked UI, five Google tools). None of them show a working result beyond the landing page. Treat them as a discovery feed, not a review.
3. **Sponsored and self-promotional content is ~30% of the set.** Explicit: Manus (#manuspartner), Granola (#GranolaPartner), Karen X Cheng (xAI/"SpaceX AI"), Fo (#wajo). Implicit: Avori (creator is the founder), Instinct (VC whose portfolio company Checkmate is in the demo), Veteran AI (branded watermark). The two most useful money items (Startups program, API credits) are from builders talking about their own apps, not ads.
4. **"Personal agent with its own identity" is the product wave of autumn 2026.** Instinct ($10B), Meta Muse (Sept 8), Dots (Sept 29), Cue (Sept 28), Fo (Sept 28), Grok Bot (Aug 11), Hark (Oct 6), Town. Every one wants Gmail, calendar and a card. Creators treat that as magic; none of them discuss the Forbes-reported Instinct retention complaint or the pre-launch Manus prompt-injection finding beyond a sentence.
5. **"Decision models" became a commodity in two weeks.** Jev (TypeSafe) went viral, OpenAI shipped the Decisions API in preview at DevDay (Sept 29) and public beta on Oct 6 at $0.10 per 1M input tokens. Three videos cover it; the useful lesson is the one @luispdoesai drew: if your product is a feature, you are one keynote away from death.
6. **Benchmark bait against Claude.** "Claude is beyond cooked" rests on DeepSeek's own harness number (87.9 vs 88.0) and ignores that Anthropic's pricing page already lists Sonnet 5.5 at $2/$10 and Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50, i.e. the "cheap Sonnet 5.5" the creator demands already exists.
7. **The Anthropic items are the only ones with deadlines.** Cloud-session credit (claim by Oct 7, spend by Nov 4), Startups program ("over capacity", re-reviewing), monthly API credits (expire every billing cycle). Everything else can wait.

## What holds up vs what is hype (with evidence)

**Holds up, verified on official pages (2026-10-10):**
- **Monthly API credits for Max/Team**: Max 5x $100, Max 20x $200, Team pooled up to $500; covers Messages/Batches API, Agent SDK, Managed Agents, Playground; excludes interactive Claude Code; 7-day wait; link one Console org at claude.ai Settings > Billing; expires each cycle, no rollover (platform.claude.com docs + support article 17154008). Haiku 5.5 is real at $0.10/$0.50.
- **Claude for Startups**: application link live (platform.claude.com/offers/startups-application), Startup Stack partners listed (ElevenLabs 12 months + 33M chars, ClickHouse $5K, Granola 3 months Business, Hex 6 months, Firecrawl 2.5x, Gamma 25%), bi-weekly office hours. But the FAQ now says verbatim: "we're currently over capacity on the Claude Team and $1,000 API credit offers" and "we'll re-review all applications which may result in a change in the status of some applications". The videos (Oct 6-8) predate this.
- **Cloud-session bonus credit**: $100 Pro / $250 Max, eligible if subscribed on Sept 23, claim by Oct 7 11:59 PM PT (closed), expires Nov 4, some accounts auto-granted, exempt from plan limits while it lasts (support article 17152539).
- **Claude Code cloud sessions**: Pro/Max/Team, no VM charge, share plan limits, `claude --cloud`, mobile Code tab, `/web-setup`, `--teleport`, PR auto-fix (code.claude.com docs).
- **Claude Motion**: launched Oct 8 2026, Team/Enterprise only, MP4 export, no `/motion` command; the composer UI in the video is a mock. Dashboards beta is on Pro/Max.
- **Repos** (stars as of today): MarkItDown 189.3k (MIT, v0.1.8), Scrapling 86.6k (BSD-3, v0.4.15), MiroFish 77.7k (AGPL), OmniRoute 74.9k (MIT, v3.8.51 Sept 30), Voicebox 56.8k (MIT, DMG installer, built-in MCP), Postiz 37.0k (AGPL, v2.25.0 Oct 2, MCP + CLI), Apify actors-mcp-server 10.4k (MIT, mcp.apify.com), open-slide 9.1k (MIT, Vercel OSS, exports PPTX/PDF). Every star count the creators quoted was accurate or already out of date on the low side.
- **Funding/launch facts**: Instinct $1B Series C at $10B (Sequoia/Benchmark/Coatue); Meta Muse Sept 8, US only, free + $20/$100; Manus 2.0 + Cue Sept 28, invite code MEETCUE; Hark Oct 6, free/$20/$100, first 100k get Pro free; Dots Sept 29, Pro-only; Grok Bot Aug 11 beta; Decisions API public beta Oct 6 at $0.10/1M input, output free; OpenAI 28-day campaign Oct 5 to Nov 2 with Codex auto-review made free on Day 2.

**Breaks or overstated:**
- "Never pay for API keys again" (NVIDIA): trial terms, ~40 RPM, credit-capped, production needs NVIDIA AI Enterprise, prompts may be used for model improvement; "OpenAI models" are gpt-oss open weights. "Plug into Claude Code" needs a gateway like OmniRoute, whose subscription-proxy mode is a ToS risk to the Max account.
- "800x faster" (Scrapling): a parser micro-benchmark vs BeautifulSoup+html5lib, not scraping throughput; Cloudflare bypass is self-claimed and a legal grey zone on sites you do not own.
- "20-second setup" (Postiz): Postgres + Redis + Temporal + platform developer-app approvals; free only when self-hosted; cloud is a 7-day trial.
- "Claude is beyond cooked" (DeepSeek V4 Pro): self-reported benchmark, unreproduced, promotional pricing ($0.435 vs list $1.74), and Anthropic already sells cheaper tiers.
- "Free" personal agents: Instinct/Cue/Fo/Hark are invite or early-access free with undisclosed future pricing; Muse free tier is metered with a card on file; Town's "$20" matches no tier (Starter is $15, credit-metered).
- "Killed Duolingo": Little Language Lessons is a ~15-language Labs experiment.
- "Cuts tokens 70%" (MarkItDown): unsourced; Claude Code already parses PDFs natively, so the win is on XLSX/PPTX/DOCX.
- Avori and the Karen Times bot: could not be found on the web at all; treat as unverified self-promotion.
- Veteran AI's "own servers", "12-96 h", "VA integration soon": vendor claims with no documentation found; pricing seen is $25/mo flat, annual requirement unconfirmed.

## How we should approach this in our own apps and workflow (concrete, ordered, with tradeoffs)

1. **Today, 10 minutes: claim the Max API credits.** claude.ai (browser) > Settings > Billing > API credits > Link organization to the Console org the apps ship from; create one workspace per app with a spend limit. This is $100-$200/month of API capacity that funds every in-app AI feature and `claude -p` batch job. Tradeoff: the link is permanent per org and the credits expire monthly, so plan monthly spend (Batches API at 50% off, Haiku 5.5 for high-volume calls). Source video: 7694322412619402510.
2. **Today, 5 minutes: apply to Claude for Startups anyway.** platform.claude.com/offers/startups-application with the product domain email. Even if the Team year and $1,000 are capped, the Startup Stack partner offers (ElevenLabs, Firecrawl, ClickHouse, Granola) are claimable and the application is in the queue for the reshaped program. Tradeoff: accepting a Team plan ends the Max credit link, and Team seats are only for orgs new to Team; decide at approval time whether Team (unlocks Motion, pooled $500) beats Max 20x ($200 + Claude Code 20x limits). Re-check 2026-11-10.
3. **Today, 1 minute: run `/claim-credit` and look at the usage menu.** If the $250 cloud-session credit was auto-granted, spend it before Nov 4 on `claude --cloud` jobs; they do not count against the Max limit while the credit lasts. Otherwise move on.
4. **This week: make cloud sessions and Routines the default execution layer.** `/web-setup` once, then plan locally in plan mode, push, and `claude --cloud "Execute docs/plan.md"`; add nightly Routines per app repo; approve from the phone. Tradeoff: parallel cloud sessions burn the shared limit faster, and repos must be on GitHub to push back.
5. **This week: adopt the three repos that remove friction without new vendors.** MarkItDown (`pipx install 'markitdown[all]'`, convert XLSX/PPTX/DOCX before reading; stdio MCP only), Voicebox (DMG, hold-to-talk dictation into Claude Code; MCP on 127.0.0.1:17493), open-slide (`npx @open-slide/cli init`, /create-slide, PPTX export; compare to the Slides artifact type and keep one).
6. **This week, only if marketing or lead-gen is on the roadmap:** Apify connector (free account, Claude Settings > Connectors > Apify; `claude mcp add --transport http apify https://mcp.apify.com`) for Google Maps/review scraping, and a 7-day Postiz Cloud trial with its hosted MCP before deciding whether to self-host. Keep LinkedIn out of it; Scrapling only for permitted targets.
7. **This week, 1 hour: build the routing/triage layer on Haiku 5.5, not on a new vendor.** 200 labelled examples, tool-use enum + 0-1 confidence, measure accuracy/latency/cost on the free credits. Only if it fails, test OpenAI /v1/decisions ($0.10/1M input). This answers Jev/Decisions API without adding a dependency.
8. **Skip, with reasons:** Manus/Cue (wallet-holding agents + prompt injection, referral-driven), MiroFish (slow, expensive, anecdotal accuracy), Town/Dots/Grok Bot/Muse (duplicate Claude Cowork + Routines, add inbox access and subscriptions), OmniRoute for the Max subscription (ban risk), NVIDIA for anything with customer data, Avori/Karen Times (unverifiable).
9. **Personal, not business:** Fo for admin calls (free, no card attached), Veteran AI only after reading its retention policy, Hark free-Pro slot on a throwaway account if at all.
10. **Strategic note to carry into product decisions:** the Jev-to-Decisions-API story and the Muse/Dots/Cue pile-up show that single-feature products die at the next platform keynote. Any app Haji ships should own a workflow or data moat, not a wrapper around one model call.

## Gaps: things they talk about that Haji has not liked yet but should look at

- **Claude Dashboards (beta)** launched with Motion on Oct 8 and, unlike Motion, is available on Pro/Max; no video covered it. Live-data dashboards from app metrics are a cheap founder reporting tool.
- **Claude Startup Stack partner offers beyond the headline** (ClickHouse $5K, Firecrawl 2.5x, Augment $100, Attention, Emergent, "View more"): the videos only named Linear/Lovable/ElevenLabs/Granola/Hex. Firecrawl in particular replaces most of the Scrapling/Apify use cases with a credited hosted API.
- **Anthropic's own cheap tiers**: Opus 5.5 ($4/$20), Sonnet 5.5 ($2/$10), Haiku 5.5 ($0.10/$0.50) were never mentioned; every "cheap model" video pointed at DeepSeek or NVIDIA instead.
- **Workspace spend limits and the Batches API** are the mechanisms that make the monthly credits stretch; no creator mentioned them.
- **Claude Code `/autofix-pr`, Projects and Routines** (official docs) are the real "agent that keeps working while you sleep", and are included on Max; the videos sold Dots, Grok Bot and Cue for the same job.
- **Security posture for personal agents**: the Instinct Gmail-retention complaint and Manus pre-launch injection research deserve a dedicated look before any agent gets a founder's inbox.
- **Decisions-API-style confidence calibration**: nobody tested whether "confidence" is calibrated; worth a small internal eval if routing ever gates money or customer messages.
